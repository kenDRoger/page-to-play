🎨 App Color Palette: Ebook to Audiobook

This palette draws inspiration from the materials and tones in the icon: the deep blue of the book cover, the warm glow of the play button, the creamy paper, and the metallic accents.
Category	Color Name	Hex Code	Purpose/Usage in App Theme
Primary	Deep Indigo	#2C466A	Main Navigation, Header/Footer, Primary Buttons. A sophisticated, rich blue that suggests quality and depth (like a classic book cover).
Accent/Action	Vibrant Amber	#E87D38	Action Buttons (Play/Pause, Convert), Active Tabs, Progress Bars. A high-contrast, energetic color that draws the eye and signifies action/audio.
Neutral/Base	Cream Paper	#F6F0E0	Backgrounds, Content Areas, Card/Container Backgrounds. A soft, inviting off-white that is easy on the eyes for reading lists and settings.
Text/Surface	Dark Slate	#4A4A4A	Primary Text, Headings, Icons. A dark gray for high readability against the Cream Paper background.
Highlight	Gilded Gold	#C7A868	Interactive Elements (Toggles, Sliders), Premium Icons, Border Accents. Used sparingly to add a touch of quality and polish (like the book's corners).

💡 Theme Application Suggestions

You can use these colors to define your app's light and potential dark themes:

    Light Theme (Based on Icon):

        Background: Cream Paper (#F6F0E0)

        Primary Elements: Deep Indigo (#2C466A)

        Action/Focus: Vibrant Amber (#E87D38)

        Text: Dark Slate (#4A4A4A)

    Dark Theme (Flipped Contrast):

        Background: A very dark version of Deep Indigo, e.g., #1A283C

        Primary Elements: Deep Indigo (#2C466A) or Gilded Gold (#C7A868)

        Action/Focus: Vibrant Amber (#E87D38)

        Text: Cream Paper (#F6F0E0) or a light gray

The Deep Indigo and Vibrant Amber combination provides a modern, high-contrast, and professional look that perfectly captures the "book meets playback" concept.
